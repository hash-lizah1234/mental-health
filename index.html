✦ PILLAR 03 • SMALL WAYS FORWARDA little at a time

You deserve care, respect, and a listening ear.

There is no single right way to feel. These gentle reminders can help you take the next small step.

01

Name what you are feeling

You don’t need perfect words. “I feel overwhelmed” is a good place to start.

02

Find one trusted person

A friend, relative, teacher, or counselor can listen. You can ask them to stay with you while you talk.

03

Take a small pause

Try a slow breath, a glass of water, or stepping somewhere quieter. Small things count.
